# Buy Google Play Store Gift Card: Which 9 Countries Support Gifting, What Are the Daily Limits, and How Fast Does Delivery Arrive?

You can buy a Google Play gift card online or in a physical store, and a digital card bought through Google Play typically reaches the recipient within the hour. Two rules decide whether that route is open to you at all: buying and sending a digital card directly through Google Play only works in nine countries, and a card can only be redeemed in the country where it was bought. If you're outside those nine, or buying for someone in another country, the digital gifting flow isn't available and you'll need to look at in-store or third-party online retailers instead, where availability varies by country.

The rest of this guide covers what the help documentation doesn't put up front: the per-country daily purchase caps, the payment methods that are blocked, what happens when a scheduled delivery goes wrong, and the error messages you're likely to hit.

## Daily purchase limits by country

## Online versus in-store: what actually changes

Buying through Google Play gives you the digital flow: pick a card, pick a value, choose email, SMS or a shareable link, and optionally pick a future delivery date. You can also schedule a delivery up to a year ahead. In-store and third-party online retail gets you a physical card or a code from a reseller, which is the only option if you're outside the nine supported countries, but you lose the built-in scheduling and the resend tools that come with a Google Play order.

One practical difference: with a shareable link, you can reopen the link from your order history and resend it if the recipient loses it. That's the most forgiving delivery method if you're unsure about the recipient's email address.

## Three payment rules that trip people up

Checkout accepts whatever payment methods are already available on your account. The restrictions sit on top of that.

**Google Play balance and Play Points can't buy Google Play gift cards.** Both are blocked for this purchase. That catches people who assume a balance is just another funding source.

**Play Points can buy specialty gift cards, with conditions.** You need the Play Store app on an Android device and a balance of at least 150 Play Points. You can cover the full cost with points or part of it, paying the rest with another method. Points can't be used if the specialty card is already discounted in a promotion, and cards bought with points are generally final and non-refundable once delivered.

**Digital gift cards don't earn Play Points**, apart from specific promotions for eligible users. Play Pass promotions also don't apply to gift card purchases.

## Delivery, resending and the mistakes you can't undo

Digital orders usually land inside an hour. When they don't, the delay can run to 24 hours, and the first thing to check is whether the email went to spam. The sender address is googleplay-donotreply@google.com.

If SMS was the delivery method, the recipient gets one message per gift. They can reply STOP to opt out of future gift delivery texts, START to resume, or HELP for support. Standard message and data rates apply, and mobile carriers can't help with a delayed or missing gift message.

To resend a card, the purchaser has to fill in the resend form and supply the Order ID, which is on the order receipt or in the payments centre. The same form fixes a typo'd recipient email. Note the limit: **you can't change the type of gift card after the order is placed.**

## Scheduled delivery: one year ahead, and hard to reverse

Scheduling is available on mobile devices in select regions, not everywhere. You can set a date up to one year from the purchase date. The date you pick shows up in the cart, the confirmation page, the email receipt and your purchase history, which makes it easy to check later.

The detail worth flagging: the card's expiration runs from the actual delivery date, not the purchase date. That's about when the scheduled order's validity starts, not about the balance going stale. Google's consumer pages state plainly that gift card balances carry no expiration date and no fees.

Once a scheduled delivery is set, you can't cancel it, reschedule it, or resend it yourself. Changes go through customer support. If you're buying for a date you might need to move, buy closer to the date instead of booking months out.

## Country lock, resale ban and the gift-card-buying-gift-card rule

A gift card can't be redeemed outside the country where it was purchased. A US card needs a US account; a UK card needs a UK account. This is the single most common reason a gift card turns out to be useless to the person who received it.

Three activities are prohibited outright:

- Selling a gift card or exchanging it for cash or another prepaid payment method.
- Using gift cards bought through Google Play for commercial purposes, including resale.
- Using a Google Play gift card to buy other gift cards or certain ineligible items.

Breaking these can lead to the redemption being blocked or action on your account without a refund. If you're buying from a reseller at a discount, you're the one carrying that risk, not the reseller.

## When the purchase fails

| Error message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "You have reached the daily spending limit for gift card purchases. Try again later." | Wait 24 hours, then retry. Check your country's cap in the table above. |
| "You don't have access to purchase gift cards at this time" | Your account isn't currently eligible to buy gift cards. |
| "You can't purchase this gift card at this time" | An inconsistency in your account details is blocking the purchase. |

The third one usually means something in your account information doesn't line up. Fix the account details before trying again rather than retrying the same order.

## Scams: the one rule that covers all of them

Google's own guidance is blunt. Anyone who is not Google and asks you to pay with Google Play gift cards for goods or services is likely running a scam. Gift cards are for buying content on Google Play and for the brands that issue specialty cards, nothing else. Never share a redemption code with anyone, including someone claiming to be verifying it, and never read a code aloud over the phone.

A related trap: cards bought through Google Play can't be used to buy other gift cards, so any request that involves buying a gift card with a gift card is a dead end by design.

## FAQ

**Can I use a gift card in a different country from where I bought it?**
No. Redemption is locked to the country of purchase, and Google states this on both the help pages and the consumer gift card pages.

**How long does delivery take?**
Usually under an hour, occasionally up to 24 hours. If it's been longer than that, use the resend form with your Order ID.

**Can I change the recipient after ordering?**
Not the card type, and not a scheduled delivery. You can resend to a corrected email address using the resend form if you mistyped it. For scheduled deliveries, contact support.

**Can I buy a gift card with my Google Play balance?**
No. Balance and Play Points are both blocked for Google Play gift cards. Points work only for specialty cards, from the Android Play Store app, with at least 150 points.

**What's the daily limit in my country?**
See the table above: $1,000 in the US, £1,000 in the UK, €855 in France, Germany and Italy, $1,400 in Australia and Canada, $18,000 MXN in Mexico, and ¥50,000 in Japan with a ¥300,000 monthly cap. All amounts are in local currency.

**Can I move the balance to another Google account?**
No, not once the code is redeemed. The exception is transferring balance from a child's account.

## How to choose

- **Gift for someone in the same country as you:** digital card, Email or SMS delivery, and keep the Order ID until they confirm it arrived.
- **Gift for someone in another country:** don't buy through Google Play. A card bought in your country won't redeem in theirs, and that's the fastest way to waste the money.
- **Buying for yourself:** choose **To yourself**, then check the balance under Payments & subscriptions.
- **Buying a third-party brand card:** specialty cards redeem with the issuing brand. If redemption fails, the issuer's support team handles it, not Google.
- **Large or bulk purchase:** check your country's daily cap first, and spread the order across days if you're near it.

Two things to confirm before you pay: the recipient's country matches the card's country, and the delivery email or phone number is correct, because a mistyped address is only fixable through the resend form, and a scheduled delivery can't be recalled at all.
